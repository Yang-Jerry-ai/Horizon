# China real estate policy and indicators profile

Use this profile when an item's primary value is a concrete development in mainland China's residential property market or housing policy.

Include enacted or formally proposed national and local housing policies; purchase, resale, mortgage, provident-fund, tax, land-supply, and developer-financing rules when they materially affect housing. Include official or otherwise well-attributed data on home prices, sales volume and value, inventory, new construction, real estate investment, land transactions, and mortgage conditions.

Prefer items that identify the issuing authority or data publisher, publication date, geographic scope, reporting period, units, and a meaningful prior-period or year-earlier comparison. A credible report about an unusual indicator movement belongs here even when it is not a policy announcement.

Do not route overseas property news, generic macroeconomic commentary, routine developer stock moves, listings, property advertisements, market forecasts without data, or recycled policy rumors here. Route a broad economy story to `finance-news` when housing is only incidental. Route a housing-specific policy or indicator story here even if it also has financial implications.
