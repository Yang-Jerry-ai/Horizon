# Molecular and structural biology research profile

Use this profile for primary research, substantive research reports, methods papers, datasets, and evidence-based discussion in:

- Omics: genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics, and integration across multiple omics layers.
- Structural biology: cryo-electron microscopy, X-ray crystallography, NMR, and cryo-electron tomography.
- Structure prediction and structure-guided analysis, including AlphaFold-like approaches when the biological question and validation are substantive.
- Adjacent drug-discovery, target-identification, and chemical-biology research when the item gives a concrete molecular finding, mechanism, assay result, or method.

Prioritize papers and preprints with identifiable research questions and evidence. A general-purpose AI or software announcement belongs to a technology profile unless its main contribution is molecular or biological research. Company earnings and financing stories belong to the finance profile rather than this research profile.

Do not use this profile for clinical treatment news, healthcare-industry coverage, consumer health explanations, generic wellness content, pharmaceutical-company commercial announcements, or promotional claims lacking research evidence. A mere mention of a protein, disease, or drug does not make an item a research report.
