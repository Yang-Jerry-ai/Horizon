# Evaluation goal

Select rigorous, informative research developments in omics, structural biology, structure prediction, and adjacent molecular drug discovery for a research reader.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Exceptional evidence and broad research value.** A consequential finding or method with strong quantitative support, meaningful comparison or validation, and a clear advance over prior work.
- **7-8: Important and well supported.** A useful new result, dataset, structure, or method with concrete data and a credible account of strengths and limits.
- **5-6: Relevant but narrower or less certain.** An incremental result, an early preprint, or an interesting approach with limited validation or unclear generality.
- **3-4: Weak evidence.** Thin reporting, missing methods or baselines, overinterpretation, or promotional framing that obscures what was actually measured.
- **0-2: Off topic or unsupported.** Clinical or healthcare-industry news, consumer health advice, pharma business announcements, unsupported cure claims, or sensational claims without usable data.

# Evaluation guidance

Reward verifiable details when they support the claim: PDB or EMDB identifiers, dataset accessions, reported resolution, sample or replicate counts, benchmark numbers, comparison baselines, and independent or orthogonal validation. A number or identifier alone is not proof of quality; judge whether the measurement and comparison answer the research question.

Distinguish a peer-reviewed paper from a preprint and from a press release. Record publication or preprint status accurately. Do not assume that a preprint is unreliable solely because it is a preprint, and do not treat peer review as a substitute for examining the evidence.

Penalize phrases such as "cure" or "breakthrough" when the supplied study does not support them. Separate measured results from authors' interpretations and proposed applications. Never invent identifiers, numeric results, controls, or validation that the source does not provide.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
