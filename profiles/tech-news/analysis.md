# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of news about AI in the pharmaceutical industry for a pharmacy student: AI drug-discovery models, AI-pharma partnerships, major business deals, and significant clinical results.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Landmark.** A new AI model that changes drug discovery practice; a major deal such as an MNC acquiring or licensing a pipeline at a high price; a pivotal trial success or failure; a major drug approval or rejection.
- **7-8: High value.** AI-pharma collaborations with disclosed terms; meaningful Phase 2/3 clinical data readouts; AI methods with convincing validation on drug-relevant problems; deals with significant disclosed value.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Early-stage partnerships without major terms; incremental AI model updates; early-phase trial results; smaller deals worth tracking.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Announcements of intent without terms or data; minor label or pipeline updates; vague "AI-powered" claims without technical substance.
- **0-2: Noise.** Stock market commentary, off-topic general AI or tech news, purely promotional content without data or deal terms.

Weight concrete numbers: deal value, patient numbers, trial phase, benchmark results. Signed deals score higher than "exploring collaboration". General AI news scores high only when the pharma application is central.

Use three to five specific topic tags (for example: AI drug discovery, licensing deal, Phase 3, MNC acquisition).

# Evaluation guidance

Consider technical depth, novelty, likely impact, source quality, relevance to software engineering and AI systems, and concrete supporting details. Treat substantive community debate as additional evidence of value, but do not equate popularity with technical importance. Do not reward exaggerated headlines.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
