# Technology news profile

Use this profile for news about AI in the pharmaceutical industry: AI models for drug discovery or biology, partnerships between AI and pharma companies, major pharma business deals (such as an MNC acquiring or licensing a pipeline for a high price), and important clinical trial results or drug approvals.

Do not use this profile for general AI news with no pharma angle, early basic research, minor regulatory updates, stock market commentary, or press releases without concrete data or deal terms.

Do not use this profile for content whose primary value is a complete long-form argument, a step-by-step tutorial, a full academic paper, finance-first market coverage, routine updates, or promotional material without substantive information.
